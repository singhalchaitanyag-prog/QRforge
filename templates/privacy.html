Privacy

This page describes what the QRForge software does with data. It is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

Static QR codes stay in your browser

When you create a static code (URL, text, Wi-Fi, contact, event, payment and so on), the code is generated on your device. What you type is not sent to our servers.

What is saved on your device

  • Style settings (colors, shapes, logo, frame) so the generator remembers your last look. Your typed content is not saved here.
  • Recent codes, created only when you download. These include the content you downloaded and a thumbnail. Use the Clear link beside "Recent codes" to remove them.
  • Brand kits you save, with your logo and style.
  • Preferences for dark mode and language.

All of this lives in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes it.

Accounts and dynamic codes

If you create an account, we store your name and email address, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), your links and landing pages, optional brand kits you choose to sync, and the hash of any API key. A session cookie keeps you logged in. It is HttpOnly and is used for nothing else. For each active session we keep the time it started and the browser's user-agent text.

If you turn on two-step verification, we store your authenticator secret encrypted, and hashes of your recovery codes (the codes themselves are shown to you once). We also keep a short activity log for your account (sign-ins, failed attempts, password and security changes, with the browser and operating system used) so you can spot anything unfamiliar. We do not store IP addresses in that log. Failed sign-in attempts are counted in memory for rate limiting and are not written to the database.

When someone scans a dynamic code, we record the time, device type, browser, operating system, the referring site's host name, a country code when the hosting provider supplies one, and a visitor hash. The hash is derived from the IP address, a secret and the date, so it rotates daily and the IP address itself is not stored. Crawlers and preview bots are not counted. You can delete a link at any time, which deletes its scan history.

If you add a webhook, each scan's details (without any IP address) are sent to the URL you provide.

Third parties

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the QR, scan-check and ZIP libraries from public CDNs (jsDelivr and cdnjs). Those providers can see your IP address when your browser fetches those files. Dynamic redirects and landing pages send visitors to the destination you chose, which has its own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can use the generator without an account. You can delete links, revoke API keys and clear local data whenever you like. To have your account deleted, contact the site operator.
